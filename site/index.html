What makes it different

Designed, not just drawn

Official covers

Last.fm, Genius, Apple and the artist's own YouTube uploads, matched on the same artist and song. Never a wrong cover.

Moves with the mood

Each song's tags set the tempo: a slow, low wave for ambient or devotional music, fast and tall for EDM or metal.

How it works

From play to profile in seconds

1

You press play

Anything that scrobbles to Last.fm works: Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, and more.

2

Ode notices

A small always-on watcher checks Last.fm every few seconds and finds the official cover straight away.

3

The card is ready

Colours, mood and cover are kept in memory, so a card is drawn in about a tenth of a second.

4

GitHub shows it

Every profile load fetches a fresh card. No stale minute, no manual refresh.

Get yours

Free, on your own Cloudflare account

About five minutes in the browser. Nothing to install, nothing to pay.

  1. Deploy to Cloudflare

    Opens Cloudflare with Ode ready to go. Sign in or create a free account.

  2. Add your Last.fm key

    Create one in two minutes at last.fm/api, and set LASTFM_USER to your username. Genius and YouTube keys are optional, for even more covers.

  3. Paste your snippet

    Fill in the form, copy the snippet into your profile README, and you're live.

Questions
Good to know

    
Does it cost anything?
No. Ode runs on Cloudflare's free plan, which cannot bill you: if a limit were ever reached, the card would pause for the day instead. Last.fm, Genius and YouTube keys are free.

    
Does it update while my GitHub profile is open?
GitHub shows images as they were when the page loaded, so the card updates on reload, and every reload is fresh. Its wave and cover keep moving while you watch.

    
Is the wave a progress bar?
No. Last.fm doesn't report where you are in a song, so the wave shows that something is playing, at the tempo of its mood, rather than inventing progress.

    
Why do I need my own Last.fm key?
Each deployment serves only its owner, with the owner's keys. Your card can't be slowed down by anyone else, and nobody else can spend your limits.

    
Which apps work?
Anything that scrobbles to Last.fm: Spotify, YouTube Music via a scrobbler, Apple Music, Pano Scrobbler, LastWave and many more.
