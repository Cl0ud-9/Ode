Yours in five minutes.

Ode runs on your own free Cloudflare account, with your own keys. Nothing to install, nothing to pay.

  1. Deploy to Cloudflare. Sign in or create a free account; Ode is set up for you.
  2. Add your Last.fm key. Make one in two minutes at last.fm/api, and set LASTFM_USER to your username.

Questions

    
Does it cost anything?
No. Ode runs on Cloudflare's free plan, which can't bill you: if a limit were ever reached, the card would pause for the day. Last.fm, Genius and YouTube keys are free.

    
Does it update while my profile is open?
GitHub shows images as they were when the page loaded, so the card updates on reload, and every reload is fresh. Its wave and cover keep moving while you watch.

    
Is the wave a progress bar?
No. Last.fm doesn't report where you are in a song, so the wave shows that something is playing, at the tempo of its mood, rather than inventing progress.

    
Where do the colours come from?
From the album art. Ode builds a Material You palette from each cover, checks every text colour for contrast, and draws the card in it. This page uses the same palette.

    
Which apps work?
Anything that scrobbles to Last.fm: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music through a scrobbler, Pano Scrobbler, LastWave and many more.

    
Why my own Last.fm key?
Each deployment serves only its owner, with the owner's keys. Nobody else can slow your card down or use up your limits.
