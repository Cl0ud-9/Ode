# Code of conduct

Ode is a small project. This page says how people are expected to treat each other in its issues, pull requests and
any other space that belongs to it.

## The short version

Be kind, be specific, and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.

## What that looks like

- Describe problems and give feedback in a way the other person can act on. "The title is cut off on a 320 px
  screen, here is a picture" helps; "this is broken" does not.
- Accept that people have different experience, first languages and time zones, and answer questions without
  making anyone feel small for asking.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Take criticism of your work as being about the work.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualised language or attention, and anything meant to shock or intimidate.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, and pressure on the maintainer to act faster than they can.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people
who keep doing so, with or without warning depending on how serious it was.

To report behaviour that worries you, contact the maintainer privately through the address on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Cl0ud-9), or, if that is not possible, open a private report through the
[security form](https://github.com/Cl0ud-9/Ode/security/advisories/new) and say it is a conduct report. Reports are
handled quietly, and the person who reports is not named.

This applies in every space that belongs to the project, and when someone is clearly speaking for it elsewhere.
